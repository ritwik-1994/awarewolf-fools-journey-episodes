How to give feedback

  1. Open an episode board and add your name.
  2. Watch the animatic, mark each shot Keep, Rework or Discuss, and note what should change.
  3. Click Send feedback, then Create on GitHub. Without a GitHub account, click Copy feedback and message it to Ritwik.
